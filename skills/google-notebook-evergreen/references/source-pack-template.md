# Source Pack Template

Use this when preparing text for NotebookLM's **Copied text** source flow.

```markdown
# Source Title

Prepared: YYYY-MM-DD
Purpose: One sentence explaining why this source belongs in the notebook.
Evidence boundary: Data range, extraction method, and what is missing.

## Core Question
What should NotebookLM help answer?

## High-Confidence Facts
- Fact with number/date/source boundary.
- Fact with definition.

## Definitions
- Term: meaning used in this notebook.

## Assumptions
- Assumption and why it is reasonable.

## Caveats
- What not to overclaim.
- What data would change the conclusion.

## Suggested Notebook Questions
1. Summarize the strongest findings and caveats.
2. What evidence is missing before making a recommendation?
3. Where could a reader misunderstand the probability or pattern?
```

Good source packs are narrow, dated, and explicit. They should make it easy for NotebookLM to cite the source and hard for it to invent missing facts.
